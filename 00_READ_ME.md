# Brat Generator — Off-Page SEO Execution Pack

This pack prepares the full off-page workflow without changing visible website content.

## Completed
- Live 2026 directory research
- Priority directory list
- Ready-to-paste submission copy
- Anchor + target-page map
- Editorial outreach opportunities
- Product Hunt/community distribution copy
- Tracking workbook

## External actions still required
Actual directory submissions, account creation, CAPTCHA, email verification, Product Hunt launch, Reddit/Pinterest/X posts, and editorial emails require external browser/account actions.

A backlink is only complete when a third-party site publishes it.

## Rules
1. Do not buy bulk/spam backlinks, PBN links, or link-farm packages.
2. Do not repeat exact-match anchor text everywhere.
3. Prefer branded, naked-URL, and natural partial-match anchors.
4. Do not add reciprocal badges/backlinks to the website without approval.
5. Prioritize relevant design/creator/tool sites over backlink quantity.
