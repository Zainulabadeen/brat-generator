## Implemented on-page controls
- Unique page titles and meta descriptions.
- One main H1 per indexable page.
- Self-referencing canonicals.
- Search-intent separation to reduce keyword cannibalization.
- Relevant H2/H3 structure and descriptive introductory copy.
- Internal links between generator, features, how-to, styles, video and blog content.
- Breadcrumb navigation on inner pages.
- Descriptive Open Graph/Twitter metadata.
- Visible FAQ content on the homepage plus matching FAQPage JSON-LD.
- Useful external authority citation on the homepage.
- No meta-keywords tag or artificial keyword stuffing.
- PageSpeed-critical lazy loading and animation reductions preserved.
