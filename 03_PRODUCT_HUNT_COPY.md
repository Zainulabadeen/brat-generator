# Product Hunt Launch Copy

**Name:** Brat Generator

**Tagline:** Create Brat-style text, album covers, memes and videos in your browser.

**Short description:**  
A free browser-based Brat-style design tool with live preview, custom colours, typography and blur controls, multiple export sizes, no signup and no watermark.

**Maker first comment draft:**  
I built Brat Generator to make the Brat-inspired visual workflow fast and simple in a browser. You can type a phrase, adjust colour, spacing and blur, choose an output size, and export without creating an account or adding a watermark. I also added a Brat Video Generator plus style and album-cover guides. Feedback on the workflow and output controls is welcome.
