## Cannibalization rules applied

- The homepage owns the broad `brat generator` query.
- The album-cover article owns the more specific `brat album cover generator` intent and contains the live image tool.
- The styles page owns colour/font/style questions instead of creating multiple thin near-duplicate landing pages during the On-Page phase.
- The how-to page owns tutorial intent; the troubleshooting article owns error/fix intent.
- The video page owns `brat video generator` and does not compete with the static-image homepage.
- Internal anchor text now points users to the page that owns the matching intent.

## SERP/content-gap findings used in this pass

Current competing Brat-generator results commonly include direct tool access, concise “what is it/how to use it” answers, Brat-green colour information, font/blur guidance, format/size controls, album-cover guidance, troubleshooting, and video-specific pages. This pass strengthens those topics on the most relevant existing pages without creating doorway-style duplicate pages.

Potential future standalone content opportunities belong to the next Content SEO phase, especially a deeper Brat colour-code resource, a dedicated Brat font/typography guide, and additional useful creation tutorials if Search Console data shows real demand.
