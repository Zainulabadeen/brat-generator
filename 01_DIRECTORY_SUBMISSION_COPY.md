## Long description
Brat Generator is a free browser-based creative tool for making Brat-inspired text graphics, album covers, memes and social-media visuals. Users can type a word or phrase, choose background and text colours, adjust condensed typography, spacing and blur, select an output size, and export the finished design without a watermark. The site also includes a Brat Video Generator, Brat Styles guide and practical tutorials. The tool works on modern desktop and mobile browsers, requires no account, and processes the current design locally in the browser.
